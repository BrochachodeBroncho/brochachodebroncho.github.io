DeadlineShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

DeadlineShield is a student productivity application that helps users organize academic tasks, monitor deadlines, and receive reminders.

2. Information We Access

When users choose to connect Google Classroom, DeadlineShield requests permission to access supported Google account and Classroom information, including course and coursework information permitted by the granted OAuth scopes.

The application may also use notification-related information, such as Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens, to support push notifications.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to support application features, including:

  • Displaying supported Classroom courses and coursework.
  • Helping users organize academic deadlines.
  • Providing task reminders and notifications.
  • Maintaining application functionality and security.

4. Google User Data

Google user data accessed by DeadlineShield is used only to provide or improve the application's user-facing features, in accordance with the permissions granted by the user and applicable Google API Services User Data Policy.

DeadlineShield does not sell Google user data. Google account access can be revoked through the user's Google Account permissions settings.

5. Storage and Sharing

Information may be processed or stored on the user's device and by services used to operate application features, such as Firebase. The specific information stored and its retention depend on the application's implementation.

Information is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the application, when authorized by the user, or when required by law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User Choices and Data Removal

Users may disconnect their Google account by revoking DeadlineShield's access in their Google Account settings. Users may contact the developer to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information.

8. Children's Privacy

DeadlineShield is intended for students. The application is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

This policy may be updated as the application changes. Updated versions will be published on this page.
